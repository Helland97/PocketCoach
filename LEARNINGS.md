# LEARNINGS

Append-only log of what worked, what didn't, and *why*. Anything that would
make future-me (or future Claude) faster on the next attempt belongs here.

**When to add an entry:** after a non-obvious fix, a failed experiment worth
remembering, a model tweak with a measurable effect, or a gotcha that ate
more than 15 minutes.

**Format:** date + one-line headline, then 2–6 lines of detail. Keep it tight.

---

## Template

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — short headline

**Context:** what I was trying to do.
**What I tried:** the approach.
**Result:** worked / didn't / partial — with numbers if applicable.
**Why:** the underlying reason (this is the part future-me actually needs).
**Next:** what to try next, or "shelved".
```
